Style options for WFS Tracker

Four decisions, two options each. Every tile below is live HTML rendered with the app's real component vocabulary — top bar, KPI cards, a stacked bar chart, calendar chips and a table — so you're judging the actual thing, not a mood board.

Each pair changes only that one dimension; everything else is held at the current baseline. Pick one per question and they combine — e.g. Warm Paper + Inter + Flat + Outline.

Question 1 of 4

Colour direction

How the whole thing feels, and how Leave and WFH read against each other.

Option A — Cool Slatecurrent Cool grey-blue neutrals. Amber leave, indigo WFH.
Reads as: standard enterprise analytics. Highest contrast of the two, strongest Leave/WFH separation, safest on a projector.
Option B — Warm Paper Ivory and sand neutrals, ink-brown text. Terracotta leave, teal WFH.
Reads as: calmer and more editorial, easier on the eyes for long sessions. Slightly lower contrast, and less shouty about status colours.
Question 2 of 4

Typography

Mostly about how the numbers read, since this is a dashboard.

Option A — Native Systemcurrent Segoe UI / system-ui throughout, with tabular figures.
Trade-off: zero network load, looks native on every machine, and matches the rest of your Windows tooling. Least distinctive.
Option B — Inter + JetBrains Mono Inter for the interface, monospace for every number and date.
Trade-off: figures line up in perfect columns and headings get tighter — more "product", less "spreadsheet". Costs a webfont download.
Question 3 of 4

Shape & density

How much fits on one screen before you scroll.

Option A — Soft Cardscurrent 10px corners, soft shadows, generous padding.
Trade-off: airy and modern, easy to scan card by card. Costs vertical space — the dashboards scroll more.
Option B — Flat Grid 4px corners, no shadows, hairline rules, tighter padding.
Trade-off: fits about 24% more per screen (measured against the tile on the left) and feels closer to the sheet you're migrating from. Denser, so slightly more work to scan.
Question 4 of 4

Chart style

Applies to the bar, column and line charts across all three dashboard tabs.

Option A — Filled Solidcurrent Every series solid, colour legend underneath.
Trade-off: all four leave categories are equally visible at once — best when you're comparing the mix. Busier, and colour does all the work.
Option B — Outline & Accent Lead series solid, the rest ghosted; values labelled inline, no legend.
Trade-off: pushes one number forward per row so the ranking is instant, and survives greyscale printing. Harder to compare the smaller categories.
